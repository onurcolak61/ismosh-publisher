Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Ismos Publisher is a local desktop publishing tool used solely by the owner of the Ismos 3D channel.

Data Collected

The application only stores OAuth2 access tokens, refresh tokens, and basic account status locally on the operator's computer to authenticate and publish approved videos to TikTok.

Data Sharing & Storage

No user data is sold, shared with third parties, or uploaded to external servers. Tokens can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings or deleted locally.
